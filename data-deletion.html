Account Deletion

Instructions for deleting your DocTube account and personal data.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

DocTube users can request the permanent deletion of their account and associated personal information.

Follow These Steps

  1. Send an email to emmlio34567@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line DocTube Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your registered phone number so we can identify your account.
  4. Our team will verify your request and process the deletion.

You do not need to reinstall the application or maintain an active subscription to request deletion.

2. Data That Will Be Deleted

Once your deletion request has been verified and processed, the following information associated with your account will be permanently deleted:

  • Your registered phone number.
  • Your account information and associated profile data.
  • Any other personal information associated with your account, where applicable.

3. Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained only when necessary to comply with applicable legal obligations or resolve disputes.

  • Information required by law may be retained for the legally mandated period.

Any retained information will be securely stored, used only for the applicable legal purpose, and deleted when it is no longer required.

Retention period: Only for the period required by applicable law.

4. Doctor Identification Images

Doctor identification images submitted for professional verification are discarded once the verification process is completed. They are not retained for ongoing use.
